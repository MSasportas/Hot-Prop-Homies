# 100 ways to get more picks a night

2026-11-12. Every idea here can be tested on data we already store. No new credits, no paid data.

**What we have saved**
- NBA: 18 nights, 4 price checks a game (11 AM, 90, 45, 20 min), Hard Rock plus 9 other books (Pinnacle, DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, ESPN Bet, BetRivers, BetOnline, and two exchanges: Novig, ProphetX), 4 prop types, box scores, injuries, lineups.
- NHL: 32 nights this season plus all of last season's Hard Rock prices, shots and points, 8 other books, goalies, line combos, sides and totals.

**What we already know**
- NHL rule (Hard Rock 3%+ better than 4+ books) beats the closing line by about 4.5% on shots and points.
- NBA prices are tight. Only 3-pointers beat the close so far. The NHL rule on NBA finds almost nothing (closed twice).

Scores: **Picks** = how many extra picks a night it could add. **Odds** = my read on the chance it beats the closing line. Both are judgment, not test results.

## The top 5

Picked for: adds real picks every night, has a reason to beat the close, and has enough saved data to test now.

| Rank | Idea | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NHL: lower the gap bar to 2 to 2.5%, shots and points** (#1, #2, #10) | The rule beats the close by 4.5%, so there is room to loosen. Last season's Hard Rock history gives a big sample to find where the edge runs out. |
| 2 | **NBA: line disagreements** (#24, #25, #26) | Today's rule only looks when Hard Rock and Pinnacle show the same line. A full point of difference in the line is a much bigger gap than a few cents of price, and nobody has tested it. |
| 3 | **NHL: more lines and fewer blocks** (#8, #9, #15, #98) | Shots 0.5 and 4.5, a shots and a points pick on one player, and a look at what the veto and cap threw away. Same proven rule, more chances. |
| 4 | **NBA: 3-pointers on a looser trigger** (#21, #22) | The only NBA market beating the close in both the live picks and the Lab. |
| 5 | **NHL: price bands across last season** (#99) | The biggest sample we own. If certain line and price bands beat the close all season, they become a second NHL rule. Needs a strict test because it has no market signal behind it. |

**Left out on purpose**
- Lean tiers and A/B picks (#93, #94): more picks, no edge. They would lower the hit rate.
- Parlays (#89 to #92): more bets, not more edge. Worth adding once the legs are proven.
- Player-situation ideas (C and F): these are out-predicting the books, which is not our lane.

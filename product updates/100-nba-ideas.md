# 100 ways to get more NBA picks a night

2026-11-12. NBA only.

**Where NBA stands**
- About 3 picks a night. Season 37-21, +14.3u, but the edge against the closing line is about zero.
- Hard Rock's main-line prices on points, rebounds, assists and 3-pointers are tight. About 30 ideas on those four markets have been tested on saved data and closed, including line disagreements today (1,028 cases, 10 worth a bet).
- So the saved data on those four markets is mostly mined out. Most real volume has to come from somewhere new: new prop types, more price checks, or a second place to bet.

**Data** column: **Saved** = testable today, no credits. **New** = needs new price pulls (credits) or a new free feed.
**Picks** = extra picks a night it could add. **Odds** = my read on the chance it beats the closing line. Both are judgment, not results.

**Left out on purpose**
- Game lines (B): cheap, but they are the sharpest markets there are. Low odds of an edge.
- Leans and tiers (#91, #92): more picks, no edge.
- News and matchup ideas (G): out-predicting the books, not our lane.
- College basketball (#95): possibly the best volume idea here, but it is a new product, not NBA.
